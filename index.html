Privacy Policy — WatchBack GainMore

Last updated 10 September 2026

WatchBack GainMore lets creators earn coins by watching and reviewing other creators' videos, and spend those coins on feedback and views for their own. This policy describes exactly what the app stores, why, who else can see it, and how to remove it. It does not describe anything the app does not do.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address Identifies your account. Supplied either by you (one-time code sign-in) or by Google (Sign in with Google).
Display handle Shown to other users beside reviews you write. Generated automatically from your account id unless you change it.
YouTube video links you submit So other users can watch or review the videos you promote. We also store the video's public title, channel name and length, which YouTube provides to anyone.
Coin balance and transaction history Every coin movement is recorded so a balance can be explained.
Watch verification events While a video plays we record playback position and player state every few seconds. This is how the app confirms a view was genuinely watched before paying for it, and how automated farming is detected.
Reviews you write Shown to the creator whose video you reviewed.
When you open the app, and which version The date and time you open the app, how many times that day, and the app's version number. Used to tell whether a problem you report is one we have already fixed, and to see whether people are finding anything worth doing when they arrive.
Device integrity result Google Play Integrity reports whether the app is the genuine version from Google Play, running on a device Google recognises. We store Google's labels for that check — for example that the device meets basic integrity, or that the app is recognised by Play — and whether it passed. We never receive or store a device identifier, hardware ID, or anything about other apps on your phone.

What we do not collect

  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, files, or messages.
  • No device identifiers — no Android ID, hardware ID, MAC address or advertising ID.
  • No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. The app shows no ads.
  • No YouTube account access. The app never signs in to YouTube, cannot see your channel, and cannot like, subscribe or act on your behalf there.
  • No payment details. Coins are an in-app score with no cash value and cannot be bought, sold, or withdrawn.

Videos are played by YouTube

Every video in the app plays through YouTube's own embedded player. While it plays, YouTube — not us — may set cookies and collect information according to Google's Privacy Policy and YouTube's terms. We do not control what YouTube collects through its player, and we receive nothing from it beyond the playback position described above.

Fair use and automatic checks

Coins are paid for real watching and real reviews, so the app checks its own work. These checks run automatically:

  • Watch verification. A view only pays if playback genuinely advanced for the required time. Skipping ahead does not count.
  • A daily earning limit on coins from watching and reviewing, so no single account can drain every campaign.
  • Device integrity, described above. At present this is recorded but does not block anyone.
  • Video availability. If a promoted video stops playing inside the app — because embedding was turned off, the video was removed, or its channel was taken down by YouTube — the campaign is stopped, the unused coins are returned to whoever posted it, and they are told why.

Accounts that farm coins with bots, scripts or modified copies of the app may be suspended, and coins earned that way may be removed. If you believe an automatic decision about your account was wrong, email us from your account's address and a person will look at it.

Who your data is shared with

We do not sell data and do not share it for advertising. It is processed by these providers on our behalf, and only for the purpose given:

  • Supabase — hosts the database and sign-in.
  • Google — Sign in with Google, Play Integrity, and the YouTube player.
  • Telegram — we receive a private daily summary of app activity, which can include tester email addresses, in our own Telegram chat. It is not posted anywhere public.

Other users of the app can see your handle, the videos you promote, and any reviews you write. They cannot see your email address.

We will disclose data to authorities only where the law requires it, or where it is needed to investigate fraud or abuse of the app.

Where your data is stored

Data is held by Supabase and processed by Google, whose servers may be in a different country from yours. Both providers apply their own safeguards for data they hold on behalf of others.

How it is protected

All data travels encrypted between the app and the server. Each account can read and change only its own records, enforced by the database itself rather than by the app. Coin movements go through server-side checks the app cannot bypass. If we ever learn of a breach affecting your data, we will tell affected users and, where the law requires, the authorities.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, give you a copy of it, or delete it. Email us from your account's address; we will confirm it is you and reply within 30 days.

Deleting your account

Open the menu and choose Delete account. This immediately and permanently removes your profile, your submitted videos, your reviews, your watch history, and your coin ledger. It cannot be undone, and coins are not refundable because they have no cash value.

Coins other people already earned for reviewing or watching your videos stay with them — their work is not undone by your account closing.

If you cannot access the app, email the address below from the account's email address and we will delete it for you. More detail is on the account deletion page.

How long data is kept

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Watch verification events and integrity results are kept so a disputed payout can be reconstructed, and are removed with the account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and surface a notice in the app before the change takes effect.
